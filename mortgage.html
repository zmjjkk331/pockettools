About this mortgage calculator

This free mortgage calculator estimates a fixed monthly loan payment from the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment term. It also shows estimated total repayment and total interest over the selected term.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose the currency used to display the result.
  2. Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan term in years.
  3. Select Calculate payment to view the estimated monthly payment, total repayment and total interest.

Frequently asked questions

Does the result include taxes and insurance?

No. The estimate excludes property taxes, insurance, lender fees and other costs.

Can I calculate a zero-interest loan?

Yes. The calculator handles a zero annual interest rate.

Does changing currency convert the loan using exchange rates?

No. The currency selector changes the display format only; it does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.
