About this tip and bill splitter

Use this tip calculator to estimate a tip from a bill amount and tip percentage. It adds the tip to the bill and divides the total evenly by the number of people.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose the currency used to display the result.
  2. Enter the bill amount, tip percentage and number of people.
  3. Select Calculate split to see the tip, total bill and amount per person.

Frequently asked questions

Can I split the bill between several people?

Yes. Enter the number of people and the calculator divides the bill plus tip evenly.

Can I change the tip percentage?

Yes. Enter any percentage you want to use.

Does the currency selector convert currencies?

No. It changes the result's currency display only and does not use live exchange rates.
