About this salary converter

This salary converter estimates equivalent gross pay across hourly, weekly, monthly and annual periods. You can set hours worked per week and working weeks per year to match your schedule.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose the currency used to display the result.
  2. Enter your pay amount and select its current pay period.
  3. Set hours per week and working weeks per year, then select Convert salary.

Frequently asked questions

Are salary results before or after tax?

The calculator estimates gross pay before taxes and deductions.

Can I change weekly hours?

Yes. Enter your usual working hours per week.

How is monthly salary estimated?

Annual gross pay is divided by 12 to estimate monthly gross pay.
