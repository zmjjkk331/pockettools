About this unit converter

This free unit converter supports common measurements for length, weight, temperature and area. Select a category, enter a value, choose the source and target units, and see the converted result.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose a measurement category: Length, Weight, Temperature or Area.
  2. Enter the value you want to convert.
  3. Choose the From and To units. The result updates as you change the value or units.

Frequently asked questions

Which categories are supported?

Length, weight, temperature and area conversions are supported.

Can I convert feet to meters?

Yes. Choose Length, select Foot as the source unit and Meter as the target unit.

Are the results rounded?

Results are displayed with up to six decimal places for readability.
