Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Overview

PocketTools is a collection of browser-based utilities. This starter version does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally send the text or images you enter into the tools to a PocketTools server.

2. Local processing

Image compression and the calculators and text utilities run in your browser. Files selected for image compression are processed locally by your browser. The resulting compressed file is generated on your device.

3. Cookies and analytics

This starter version does not include advertising cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party tracking code. Your hosting provider may process standard technical request information when you visit the website; consult that provider's privacy documentation.

4. Third-party services

If third-party advertising, analytics, external APIs, or other services are added later, this policy must be updated before those services are activated.

5. Children's privacy

This website is a general-purpose utility site and is not specifically directed to children.

6. Contact

No dedicated support email has been configured for this starter release. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the site.
