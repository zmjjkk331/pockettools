About this text toolkit

The online text toolkit provides quick text utilities in your browser. It counts words, characters, characters without spaces and lines, and includes uppercase, lowercase, whitespace cleanup and copy actions.

How to use this tool

  1. Type or paste text into the text box.
  2. Review the word, character and line counts, which update as you type.
  3. Use the buttons to change letter case, clean extra spaces or copy the text.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a word?

Words are counted as groups of non-whitespace characters separated by spaces or line breaks.

Does the character count include spaces?

The toolkit shows both total characters and characters without whitespace.

Is my text uploaded?

The text operations run in your browser; the text is not sent to PocketTools.
